## Slide-by-slide confirmed content

1. **Campaign cover** — Mirchi presents “Vriksha Vvandhan” with the line “This Raksha Bandhan Protect the Protector.” Visual direction: warm sunlight, mature tree bark, human hands and a Rakhi.
2. **Raksha Bandhan context** — The festival celebrates people who protect us. The deck mentions brothers, soldiers, pets and vehicles as examples of gratitude and protection.
3. **The unthanked protector** — A tree outside the home is framed as a quiet protector associated with shade, cleaner air, cooler streets and “countless breaths.” The slide also contains a disappearance claim that is not used on the website because it is unsourced.
4. **Core campaign tension** — “The protector has always protected us. it’s time to protect the protector.” The website preserves the approved wording with sentence-case punctuation.
5. **Campaign invitation** — Mirchi invites India to tie a Rakhi to a tree as a promise to protect, nurture, and see the tree as more than “just another tree.”
6. **Hindi narrative line** — “Har saal hum uski kalai dhoondte hain jo humein bachata hai… Shayad is saal… humein uski jad dhoondni chahiye.” This confirms the root/wrist wordplay and a ceremonial, emotional tone.
7. **The Mirchi movement** — “983 Trees. One Frequency. Infinite Promises.” The deck describes 98.3 Mirchi beginning with Rakhis tied to 983 city trees and gives each tree “A Rakhi. An identity. A protector.”
8. **The First Rakhi Moment** — The movement begins with public figures Sidharth Malhotra and Tamannaah Bhatia tying a Rakhi to a tree with personal meaning. The deck contains a still concept, not an approved website video file.
9. **Digital #VrikshaVvandhan** — Suggested social stories include tying a Rakhi, hugging trees, childhood neighbourhood-tree memories and before/after stories. A meet-and-greet is mentioned without rules or legal details.
10. **On-ground campaign** — Mirchi RJs visit parks, schools, colleges and housing societies, inviting listeners to tie eco-friendly Rakhis and pledge care. The slide uses “983 Trees. 983 Promises.”
11. **Participation journey** — Tie a Rakhi; visit the website; upload a tree photo; receive a personalised digital certificate. The concept certificate says “Guardian of Tree No. 417.” Supporting copy says the live tracker can continue beyond 983.
12. **Ped Ka Paigaam** — Mirchi RJs voice messages from a tree’s point of view. Sample: “Tumhare bachpan ka har raaz mujhe yaad hai… school ka pehla din, pehla pyaar, pehli selfie… sab dekha hai maine.”
13. **983 Tree Tracker / website** — A live on-air countdown and exclusive website are proposed. The example tracker reads `417 / 983`; the deck includes a Mumbai city image and a Hindi on-air update.
14. **Campaign conclusion** — “One Rakhi. One Story. Millions inspired to protect a tree.”
15. **Deliverables and dates** — A working plan references pre-promotion on 21–23 August, campaign activity on 24–28 August, RJ mentions, promos and digital posts. No year or final approval status is confirmed.
16. **Closing cover** — Repeats the campaign cover and “Thank You.”
