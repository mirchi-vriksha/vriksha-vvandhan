# Launch content approval

No item below is inferred. Company legal/content owners must change each PENDING item to APPROVED (with approver/date/version) or NOT APPLICABLE before public launch.

| Decision | Status | Required evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Final campaign terms | PENDING | Approved copy/version and effective date |
| Privacy wording | PENDING | Approved notice, controller/contact and lawful basis |
| Publication consent wording/version | PENDING | Approved explicit consent text and withdrawal process |
| Media/photo rights | PENDING | Approved participant licence/rights language |
| Retention duration and deletion process | PENDING | Database, originals, publications, certificates, exports and logs schedule |
| Minors policy | PENDING | Eligibility, guardian consent and moderation handling |
| Geography/eligibility | PENDING | Approved territories and restrictions |
| Campaign start date/time | PENDING | Time zone and opening owner |
| Campaign end date/time | PENDING | Closing behaviour and archive plan |
| Behaviour at/after 983 | PENDING | Option A continue, B auto-close, or C Admin-close |
| Email sender identity | PENDING | Company domain/display name/reply-to approval |
| Certificate wording and signature | PENDING | Final visual, name/date/Guardian text and authorised signatory |
| Data export handling policy | PENDING | Approved users, storage, sharing and deletion controls |

Operational recommendation for 983 is to keep accepting and allow a count above 983 unless the company approves another option. The application and visual counter support values above 983; no automatic close is implemented.
